## About ISO 27001

I think of ISO 27001 as a structured way for an organisation to protect their information. It’s a standardized set of instuctions (global standard)  that tells a company on how to manage their security risks properly, stay organised and on top of things, and making sure nothing important slips through the cracks.

It doesn’t just focus on technology — it covers people, processes, and systems. In simple terms, it helps a company build an Information Security Management System (ISMS), which is both standardized and repeatable. It is a well‑documented way of keeping information safe.
